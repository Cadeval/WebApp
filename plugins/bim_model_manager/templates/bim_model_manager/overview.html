The BIM Workspace

Bring building models, material data and assessment reports together in one workflow.

Models and 3D inspection

Upload IFC or CityJSON files, explore their geometry and inspect element properties and materials. Select a grouped validation warning to find its affected IFC elements in the viewer.

Materials, impacts and recovery

Assess material quantities and environmental impacts, create material passports and compare saved reports. Review costs in EUR and how recovery grades change the estimates. Missing properties and validation warnings remain visible alongside the results.

Maps and local context

Locate buildings on the map and look up energy and utility pricing or planning information, starting with Austria and Vienna. Provider dates and coverage help you judge whether the information applies to your building.

Reference data and reports

Choose the material and assessment references your project uses, review calculation progress and export results. Saved models and reports stay within the permissions of your workspace.
