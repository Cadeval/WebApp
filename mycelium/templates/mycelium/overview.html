What is Cadevil?

Cadevil is an open-source building assessment platform for architects and engineers. Developed from a master's thesis project, it helps you understand and compare buildings using their IFC models.

The Mycelium plugin framework

Mycelium connects the tools in Cadevil. Each plugin contributes its own workflow, pages and capabilities, so you can build a workspace around the work you need to do.

Your account keeps your chosen tools together. User settings let you manage your profile, preferences and signing keys; administrators also manage users and groups.
