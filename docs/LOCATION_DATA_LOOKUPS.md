# Location data in the building map

Select a marker or a building in **Building Map**, then choose **Local data & rules**. The results open in the map without changing its URL. List links also open a full-page view. Refresh uses the same authenticated workflow and CSRF protection as the existing pages.

The server resolves the selected building from an owned upload. It uses the effective map coordinates: an owner-set marker overrides a matching CityJSON geometry anchor, which overrides the IFC source location. Query-string coordinates or provider URLs cannot replace this location. Missing locations and service failures leave the viewer and location editor usable. Responses are private and not stored in the browser cache. Results for a previously selected building cannot replace the current selection's panel.

## Energy and utilities

The [official aWATTar Austria API](https://www.awattar.at/services/api) supplies EPEX Spot day-ahead electricity prices for the Austrian bidding area. These are country-wide wholesale values, not postcode-specific household tariffs. The adapter converts EUR/MWh to EUR/kWh exactly, preserves negative values, validates overlapping/duplicate intervals and displays offset-aware Europe/Vienna times, including daylight-saving transitions. It reselects the current interval from cached data on every lookup; it does not extrapolate missing prices or turn an hourly quote into an annual bill. Supplier charges, grid charges, levies and VAT are excluded. [E-Control's tariff calculator](https://www.e-control.at/tarifkalkulator) supplies the separate retail comparison using postcode and annual consumption; its restricted integration API is not scraped.

Official Vienna municipal tariffs are versioned source snapshots, with the authority's effective period separate from Cadevil's verification date and review deadline. Water/sewer charges require a municipal-service connection; water meter fees are additional. The waste example identifies its bin volume and collection frequency. An expired or not-yet-effective snapshot supplies no numeric current price. Gas and district heating require an individual tariff/connection/contract, so their entries link to official sources and list the necessary inputs without inventing a universal charge. These values are not silently written into existing material/LCA reports.

Country routing uses the bundled public-domain [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) Austria polygon solely to select an energy provider. Points within 1 km of its generalized boundary are not assigned national prices automatically. This routing geometry is not a legal or cadastral boundary. Planning jurisdiction is determined separately by official municipal polygons.

The municipal snapshot links to [Vienna's water and sewer fees](https://www.wien.gv.at/umwelt/eu-trinkwasserrichtlinie) and [residual-waste tariff table](https://www.wien.gv.at/umwelt/hausmuellgebuehren-tarifuebersicht). Its published schedule starts on 2025-01-01, was verified on 2026-10-03 and has no authority-supplied end date. Cadevil stops displaying current numeric amounts after its separate 2026-12-31 review deadline. Numeric charges require a verified interior Vienna district result; unknown or boundary locations keep the source references. [Wien Energie's district heating models](https://www.wienenergie.at/privat/produkte/waerme/fernwaerme/) and the E-Control gas calculator remain contract lookups.

## Vienna planning context

The provider queries the City's fixed HTTPS WFS and WMS services. It filters `BEZIRKSGRENZEOGD` and `GENFLWIDMUNGOGD` candidates using their actual polygons, including holes. Bounding-box candidates alone never establish a match. Polygon boundaries, multiple matches and truncated responses are labelled or rejected rather than selecting an arbitrary parcel. `SCHNITTMUSTEROGD` map identification supplies planning document numbers and publication dates, with links to both plan and text.

Generalized zoning is informational. Building-class height ranges from [Bauordnung §75](https://ris.bka.gv.at/eli/lgbl/WI/1930/11/P75/LWI40013122) are emitted as `site_specific=false`, not as the maximum allowed on the model's parcel. The plan, §75 qualifications and [§81 measurement provisions](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/lgbl/WI/1930/11/P81/LWI40018199) may change the permitted height. Setbacks, coverage, building lines and special provisions remain explicit missing information until a provider can establish them. The IFC bounding-box height is never compared against a legal cap. Plan publication and source retrieval are not treated as effective dates. Read the [official City planning guidance](https://www.wien.gv.at/stadtplanung/flaechenwidmung-bebauungsplan-hilfe) and the linked plan/text together.

Austria-wide energy routing is available; municipal planning and numeric service tariffs initially cover Vienna. Other Austrian municipalities show the coverage limit and need a separate provider. Approximate IFC site origins are clearly marked and should be replaced with the building's actual position before relying on local planning information.

## Caching and operations

`LOCATION_LOOKUP_CACHE_ROOT` defaults to `BASE_DIR/data/location-lookup-cache`, outside public media/static storage. Keep it private. Provider snapshots and lock files contain source data, not user/model IDs. Vienna receives the queried marker's small bounding box; the national electricity feed receives only a time window and no building coordinates, address or model file.

Cross-process locks prevent duplicate lookups across Bolt workers. Austrian electricity shares one national cache: one-hour fresh lifetime and at least 20 minutes between any provider attempts, including forced refresh and failures. This stays below the provider's documented 100 requests/day fair use for a single shared cache. Planning uses exact coordinate keys, a 24-hour fresh lifetime and a five-minute retry floor. Partial failures retry after that floor and preserve a previously complete snapshot with a saved-data notice. Electricity older than one day and planning older than seven days no longer expose saved numeric/source results as current.

Fixed allowlisted endpoints, HTTPS certificate verification, disabled redirects/proxies, bounded unbuffered responses and network timeouts protect the lookup boundary. XML entity/DOCTYPE declarations and unexpected JSON/CRS/geometries are rejected. The response budget is six seconds plus at most the two-second read timeout per provider request. A failed cache does not bypass provider rate limits. The native routes retain login, ownership, user workflow selection and CSRF; this is business data functionality compatible with production, not an MCP server.

To add another region, implement a provider with its verified boundary, units, effective dates, source URLs, finite bounded values and explicit unsupported/partial states. Give it an exact scope and private cache key. Do not substitute neighbouring parcel records, wholesale prices for retail tariffs, or generalized map labels for building permission.
